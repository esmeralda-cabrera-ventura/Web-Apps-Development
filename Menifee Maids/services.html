Common questions

Before you book.

How much does house cleaning cost in Menifee?

It depends on workload, the size of the area, and how many cleaners are needed. The minimum is $150 per visit. The quote tool on our booking page gives you an exact number in about a minute.

Which cities do you serve?

Fifteen cities across western Riverside County, including Menifee, Murrieta, Temecula, Wildomar, Lake Elsinore, Hemet, San Jacinto, Corona and Riverside. We're mobile and always travel to you.

Do you handle offices and construction sites?

Yes — office cleaning and janitorial contracts for businesses, plus post-construction cleanup for builders and contractors.

Are your cleaners background checked?

Yes. Every cleaner is a trained professional who has passed an extensive background check before hiring. We're licensed, family-owned and veteran-owned.
