ABOUT ME

I’m the kind of technologist who loves taking a massive, seemingly impossible operational puzzle and turning it into an elegant, scalable digital reality. As a security-cleared Solutions Architect, Software Engineer, and Founder of Apex Intelligent Systems, I spend my time crafting custom cloud infrastructure, full-stack software, and AI-driven automation systems that transform how organizations operate. Client-obsessed and fiercely customer-focused, I design every system with an uncompromising eye on the end-user experience.

I combine deep systems-level strategy with the hands-on passion of an active engineer. I believe great technology shouldn’t just function, it should seamlessly bridge operations, strategy, and continuous innovation to give organizations a serious competitive edge.

My work spans architecture through implementation, including the design of production-grade Azure, AWS, and hybrid cloud environments, as well as the development of AI applications, agentic AI workflows, automation solutions, and cloud-native web applications. I combine cloud engineering, software development, Agile, DevOps, and applied machine learning to build secure, scalable, high-availability systems. My focus is on strengthening operational resilience, improving decision-making, streamlining processes, enabling modernization, and delivering real-world business outcomes across government and private-sector environments.
